# Deconstruct Source Command (`/deconstruct-source`)

## Purpose
Deconstructs a given reading passage, transcript, or article into three deep linguistic layers for IELTS Band 7.0+ mastery.

## Execution Steps
1. **Analyze Lexical Roots & Nuance:** Identify 5-10 high-value collocations or academic terms, explaining their precise shades of meaning and contextual usage.
2. **Deconstruct Syntactic Structures:** Extract advanced grammatical patterns (e.g., cleft sentences, inversion, participial phrases) that contribute to stylistic flow.
3. **Evaluate Rhetorical Devices:** Examine how the author structures arguments, transitions between ideas, and builds logical coherence.
4. **Practical Application:** Provide examples of how to apply these structures to IELTS Writing Task 2 or Speaking Part 3.
