---

## 1. Core Concept

**Complex sentences are not decorations.** They exist for ONE reason: to show the *relationship between two ideas*. If a sentence does not show a relationship, it should stay simple.

- IELTS GRA rewards **a mix of simple and complex** — not maximal complexity.
- A wrong complex sentence scores **less** than a correct simple one.
- Band 5.5–6.0 = accurate simple sentences + **a few** naturally-used complex sentences.

---

## 2. The Relationship-Word Toolkit (safety order)

Only 5 words — master these before touching `which` / `whose` / participles:

| Idea A + Idea B | Relationship | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| I'm moving to your city / I want to set up an agency | **to** (mục đích) | "I'm moving to your city **to set up** an agency." |
| I'll live there / my team works there | **because** (lý do) | "I'll live there **because** my team works there." |
| I need a flat / I move there | **before** (thời gian) | "I need a flat **before** I move there." |
| You're free / we can meet soon | **if** (điều kiện) | "**If** you're free, we can meet soon." |
| I'm excited / I'm a bit nervous | **although** (đối lập) | "**Although** I'm excited, I'm a bit nervous." |

### Safe advanced upgrades (100% risk-free)
- **`to + infinitive`** for purpose → *"I'm writing **to invite** you..."*
- **`which` + one fact** (with comma) → *"I'll arrive next month, **which** gives us time to plan."*
- **`so`** for result → *"It's cheap, **so** I can visit often."*

---

## 3. Why Broken Complexity Fails

From the personal letter draft:
> *"until I decide to found a small studio agency specify administration making"*

This smears **4 relationships** into one sentence → reader cannot follow → GRA AND Task Achievement both drop.

**THE RULE: one relationship per sentence.** Two relationships → split into two sentences.

**The comma test:** if you can't explain *why* there's a comma, split the sentence.

---

## 4. The 3-Step Drill (make complexity come OUT of meaning)

**Step 1 — Write short, true sentences** (ignore beauty):
> I'm moving to your city. I want to open an agency there. My team works there. We met for coffee last year.

**Step 2 — Merge ONE pair at a time**, using only the 5 words:
> I'm moving to your city **because** I want to open an agency there. My team works there, **so** we met for coffee last year.

**Step 3 — Stop. ✅** Three sentences, two merges, zero risk.

Repeat 20× with different topics (travel, study, gym, work) before touching advanced structures. When a structure arrives through this drill, it lands in the right place naturally.

---

## 5. Homework Drill

Two simple ideas:
1. *You are planning to move to a new city.*
2. *You want a part-time job there.*

Write **5 different versions** using each relationship word once:
- `to` / `because` / `before` / `if` / `although`

Submit versions for feedback.

- **Linguistic Struggle:** "Complex" is mistakenly treated as vocabulary, not structure-with-reason.
