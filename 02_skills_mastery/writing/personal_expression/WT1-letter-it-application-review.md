## 4. Version A — Band 5.5–6.0 (Safe, Natural)

> Dear HR Manager,
>
> I am writing to apply for the part-time IT position that I saw advertised on your social media page. My name is Phan Huu Binh Nguyen, and I am a third-year IT student at the University of Management and Technology in Ho Chi Minh City.
>
> I am very interested in this role because it is closely related to my future career, and I hope to learn more in a professional IT environment.
>
> I also have relevant experience. I currently work on my university's Red Team, which has a selective screening process, and I am a project manager for a research article team of six members. Because of these experiences, I am confident that I can handle this position and solve technical problems effectively.
>
> Thank you for considering my application.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Nguyen

## 6. Vocabulary Note — "Selective Screening Process"

- **Nghĩa tiếng Việt:** Quy trình sàng lọc khắt khe / tuyển chọn gắt gao.
- **Phân tích từng thành phần:**
  - **screening** (noun): quá trình sàng lọc, kiểm tra đầu vào.
  - **process** (noun): quy trình.
  - **selective** (adjective): có chọn lọc, khắt khe — chỉ nhận cá nhân xuất sắc qua nhiều vòng (≠ ai nộp cũng đậu).
- **Ngữ cảnh câu gốc:** *"My background includes active membership in my university's **selective Red Team**..."* → nói lên rằng vào đội Red Team phải qua sàng lọc gắt gao mới được kết nạp.
- **Vì sao tốt hơn:** diễn đạt chuyên nghiệp, thể hiện năng lực cá nhân hơn hẳn câu đơn giản *"they choose students carefully."*
- **Collocations liên quan:** selective admission · rigorous screening process · candidate screening · screening round.
