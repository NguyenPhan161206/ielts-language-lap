## 1. Task

> You saw an advertisement for a part-time café position. Write a letter to the café manager:
> - Introduce yourself.
> - Explain why you are interested in the job.
> - Describe your relevant experience.

**Type:** Formal (recipient = manager), purpose = Job Application.

## 3. Assessment

| Criterion | Band | Key problem |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| Task Achievement | 4.5 | Bullets present but experience paragraph thin/unclear |
| Coherence & Cohesion | 4.0 | One giant paragraph; no skeleton |
| Lexical Resource | 4.0 | 7+ spelling errors (*writed, creater, Univercity, opotunity, alway, thirt*) |
| Grammatical Range & Accuracy | 4.0 | "which was writed", "So that I send", "it deeply relate", "I alway get ready finding" |
| **Overall** | **4.0** | Register slips informal (*I'm* in formal letter) + broken spelling |

### Line-by-line fixes

| Original | Fix |
|----------|-----|
| Duplicate greeting | **"Dear Café Manager,"** (one only) |
| "I'm" | **"I am"** (no contractions in formal) |
| "your post ... which was writed to recruit" | **"the part-time content creator position you advertised online"** (cut dead relative clause) |
| "So that I send you this email" | **"I am writing to apply for this position"** |
| "hope you see my effort to attract your attention" | **"I hope you will consider my application"** |
| "Univercity of Management of Technology" | **"University of Management and Technology, Ho Chi Minh City"** |
| "making social media for Media scocial of school also" | **"I am currently in charge of the social media for my university's media team."** |
| "I'm at thirt year" | **"I am in my third year"** |
| "I alway get ready finding opotunity" | **"I am always ready to find opportunities"** |
| "I commit to work ... for 2 hours a day" | **"I am available to work two hours every day"** |
| "it deeply relate to my career that I enthusiastically study" | Split → **"it is closely related to my career, which I study and practice every day"** |
| "Sincerely Nguyen" | **"Yours faithfully, Nguyen"** |

## 4. Version A — Band 5.5–6.0 (Safe, Natural)

> Dear Café Manager,
>
> I am writing to apply for the part-time content creator position at Ca Coffee, which I saw advertised online. I am Phan Huu Binh Nguyen, a third-year Marketing student at the University of Management and Technology in Ho Chi Minh City.
>
> I am very interested in this job because content creation is exactly the career I am working toward. I study marketing and digital communication every day, and I am always ready to learn new skills.
>
> I also have relevant experience. I am currently in charge of the social media content for my university's media team, where I create posts and manage the page. This has taught me how to produce engaging content and cooperate with a team.
>
> I am available to work two hours every day and can start as soon as you need me. I would be happy to discuss my application in person.
>
> Thank you for considering my application.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Nguyen

## 5. Version B — Band 7.0 (Professional, Precise Collocations)

> Dear Café Manager,
>
> I am writing to apply for the part-time content creator position at Ca Coffee, which I came across in your recent online advertisement.
>
> Currently in my third year of a Marketing degree at the University of Management and Technology in Ho Chi Minh City, I have spent the past two years producing social media content for my university's official media team. This role has taught me how to turn a brand's message into engaging posts and how to collaborate effectively under deadlines — skills I believe are directly relevant to your café.
>
> What attracts me to this position is the opportunity to combine my academic knowledge with practical, everyday work. I study content strategy and digital communication daily, and I am eager to apply these ideas in a real business environment rather than simply in the classroom. I am confident that my background would allow me to contribute from the first week.
>
> I am available to work two hours each day and can adapt my schedule around your needs. I would welcome the chance to discuss my application in an interview at your convenience.
>
> Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Nguyen

---

## 6. Upgrade Comparison (A → B)

| Feature | A (5.5–6.0) | B (7.0) | Skill |
|---------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Opening | "which I saw advertised online" | "which I came across in your recent online advertisement" | Collocation (*came across*) + noun |
| Intro | "I am Phan..., a third-year..." | **Participial opener**: "Currently in my third year..., I have spent..." | Fronted structure |
| Interest | "because it is exactly..." | "**What attracts me to this position is** the opportunity to combine..." | Noun-clause subject |
| Closing | basic happy/learn | *adapt, contribute, at your convenience* | Modality + professionalism |

## Research Journey
- **Why I'm researching this:** Formal application letters = highest-frequency GT Task 1 type; register is the first band.
- **Linguistic Struggle:** Spelling errors outweigh grammar problems at this level — accuracy first.
- **Aha Moments:** Killing useless relative clauses ("your post which was writed...") instantly improves clarity.
- **Career Connection:** Job application letters mirror real professional emails — reusable with `reaserching-diary` career goals.
