## 1. Task & Original Draft (Raw)

**Task:**
> You are planning to move to a new city for work. Write a letter to a friend who lives there:
> - Tell your friend about your plans.
> - Ask for advice about living in the city.
> - Arrange a time to meet.

**📌 Type:** Informal letter (friend), purpose = Providing Information / Request.

**Draft Expression (Raw) — 144 words:**
> Dear Phong,
> Hi bro, long time no see. There are good new for us that I will come to your city and live there. I'm going to organize an agency there because I have my team live there and work together for along time until I decide to found a small studio agency specify administration making. Are there any recommendation for me? Like I should come first or what I should buy be for I come there? Oh! How long have'n we drunk some coffee and watched sunset until darkness come. For a month, I will come to 23, 39 street, Binh Trung, district 2, Ho Chi Minh city, where I will live. Is that near by you? I so expect to see you there. let's set a meet at 9:00 am, 9/23. Tell me the new time if you busy please.
> Your friend,
> Nguyen

|----------|-------|-----|
| "There are good new for us" | number/countable | "I have good news" (`news` uncountable) |
| "I will come to your city and live there" | vague | "I'm moving to your city" |
| "organize an agency" | bad collocation | "set up an agency" |

## 3. Refined Expression (Native Academic/Register: Natural Informal English)

Band 5.5–6.0 version — 149 words:

> Dear Phong,
>
> Long time no see! I have some great news — I'm going to move to your city for work. I've decided to **set up a small agency** there, because my team already lives in the city and we've worked together for a long time.
>
> Could you give me some **advice about living there**? For example, should I visit the city first, and what should I prepare before I move? I'll be staying near 23/39 Street, Binh Trung, District 2 — is that a good area?
>
> I really hope we can meet soon. I'm arriving next month, so let's arrange a time — how about **9 a.m. on 9/23**? If you're busy, just let me know when works for you.
>
> Can't wait to see you again!
>
> Your friend,
> Nguyen

**What changed:**
- 3 paragraphs = 3 bullets (plans / advice / meeting) → CC ↑
- One idea per sentence → GRA ↑
- Natural relationship words only: `to`, `because`, `so`, `if` → safe complexity
- Kept informal register: "Long time no see!", "Can't wait"

## Research Journey
- **Why I'm researching this:** Informal letters are the easiest TA wins at Band 3.5–5.5 — register + 3 bullets.
- **Linguistic Struggle:** Crushing multiple ideas into one sentence in an attempt to sound advanced destroyed meaning.
- **Aha Moments:** The letter skeleton (opening → body per bullet → closing) solves clarity before vocabulary does.
- **Career Connection:** Writing clear reports/emails = same discipline as writing clean code: one responsibility per function/sentence.
